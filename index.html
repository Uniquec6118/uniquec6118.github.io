💡 magsafe-dark - Darken your MagSafe light for focus

Download magsafe-dark

📋 About this app

The magsafe-dark utility performs one specific function. It manages the light on your MagSafe charging cable. Apple Silicon MacBooks often keep this LED indicator lit while the device charges. This light can prove distracting, especially in low-light conditions or during late-night work sessions. This application allows you to toggle that light off with one click.

Once you install the app, it places a tiny icon in your menu bar. You control the state of your charging light through this simple interface. The app requires no background processes and uses minimal system resources.

🛡️ System Requirements

  • Apple Silicon MacBook (M1, M2, M3, or later models).
  • macOS version 12.0 or newer.
  • No additional hardware or cables.

🚀 How to install

Follow these steps to set up the software on your machine:

  1. Visit the official releases page to select the latest version of the installer.
  2. Download the provided file to your Downloads folder.
  3. Double-click the downloaded file to begin the setup process.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to move the application into your Applications folder.
  5. Open the application from your Launchpad or Applications folder.

The application icon will appear in the top right corner of your screen within the menu bar.

⚙️ Usage guide

Click the magsafe-dark icon in your menu bar to view the control options. You will see a toggle switch. Click the switch to deactivate the LED on your MagSafe charger. Your MacBook will continue to charge normally, but the orange or green light will remain dark. Click the switch again to restore normal behavior.

The app remembers your preference. If you prefer the light to stay off, the app will maintain this setting even after you restart your computer.

🔧 Troubleshooting

If the application does not appear in the menu bar, ensure you have moved the file to the Applications folder and launched it. If the charging light remains on, verify that your MacBook has an Apple Silicon processor and that your MagSafe charger is firmly connected to the charging port.

Users who encounter permission requests during the first launch should grant access. macOS needs this permission to communicate with system hardware components like the power controller.
